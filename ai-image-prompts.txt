SquareCampus — image generation prompts (ChatGPT / DALL-E / any image model)
=============================================================================

GLOBAL STYLE (prepend to every prompt)
--------------------------------------
Editorial documentary photography of Indian school environments. Natural
light, realistic, warm but restrained color grading, shallow depth of field.
Composition with generous negative space on one side (text panels get
overlaid in the design). NO text, NO logos, NO watermarks, NO signage with
readable words anywhere in the frame. Faces either not visible, softly out
of focus, or turned away — no identifiable individuals. No fantasy or
illustration styles; this must read as a real institution photographed for a
serious publication.

IMPORTANT — what NOT to generate:
- No fake software screenshots or dashboards (product UI is code-rendered;
  AI-generated UI would be a fabricated product image and violates our
  claims policy).
- No awards, certificates, rankings, or "achievement" imagery.
- No stock-photo clichés: no handshakes over desks, no lightbulbs, no
  jumping graduates.

After generating: convert to .webp, place at the path given, then wire the
file into the `image:` fields in src/content/blog/posts.ts (fields are
already typed; hero uses 1200x675, section images use 800x450).

Only posts still missing images are listed below. Prompts for the sixteen
earlier posts were removed once their images shipped (see git history).
